ALEX: Before we get to our scenarios, let’s step back and learn something that’s been hiding behind our USD exposure—**the currency carry trade**.

SAM: The carry trade? I know it’s about borrowing cheap and investing in a higher‑yielding currency, but how does that connect to our portfolio?

ALEX: Here’s the mechanism. When you hold a USD‑denominated asset, you’re effectively **long USD** and **short CAD**. If the U.S. dollar yields, say, 5 % in Treasury yields while the Canadian dollar’s short‑term rates sit at 2 %, you’re earning a **2 % carry** on the notional amount—provided the exchange rate stays flat. That’s the classic carry trade: you profit from the interest‑rate differential, not from price moves in the underlying asset.

SAM: So with $232,505 CAD of USD exposure, we’re sitting on a pretty big carry opportunity, right? What does that look like in dollar terms?

ALEX: Exactly. Take the **$232,505 CAD** USD exposure. If the U.S. 2‑year yield is 5 % and the Canadian 2‑year yield is 2 %, the annual carry is **3 % of the USD‑denominated notional**. Converting that to CAD at the current 1.3834 rate, the notional is **$232,505 CAD / 1.3834 ≈ $168,000 USD**. Three percent of $168,000 USD is **$5,040 USD**, which at today’s rate equals **≈ $6,970 CAD** per year, or roughly **$58 CAD per day**.

SAM: That sounds nice, but what if the CAD appreciates? Doesn’t that wipe out the carry?

ALEX: It does. The carry trade’s profit is the **net of the interest differential minus the FX movement**. Our anchor tells us that a 0.01 CAD move in the USD/CAD rate shifts the portfolio by **$2,325 CAD**. If the CAD strengthens by 0.02 CAD over a month, that’s a **$4,650 CAD** loss, easily dwarfing the **≈ $1,740 CAD** carry earned in the same period. That’s why we recommended a **CAD/USD forward hedge covering $100 K CAD** of USD exposure—to lock in the carry while limiting translation risk.

SAM: So the practical takeaway is: the carry can add a nice buffer, but only if we hedge the FX risk, otherwise we could lose more than we earn.

ALEX: Precisely. For a growth‑focused 24‑year‑old with a massive 3× leverage tilt, the carry trade can be a low‑volatility source of return that offsets cash‑drag, provided the hedge is in place. That’s why we’re deploying the forward contract next week and monitoring the USD/CAD trend closely.
